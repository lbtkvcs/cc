# RITMAI — Poster Series

**Category:** Poster / Cultural Campaign  
**Type:** Fictional concept

## Brief
Develop a poster family for a contemporary music and movement festival.

## Direction
Large typographic blocks interact with biomorphic shapes. Each poster keeps the same typographic rules while changing the composition, making the series recognizable without becoming repetitive.

## Deliverables
- 3-poster family
- Event lock-up
- Social crops
- Digital ticket artwork
