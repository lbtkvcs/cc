# NORA — Brand Identity

**Category:** Branding / Visual Identity  
**Type:** Fictional concept

## Brief
Create a restrained identity for a modern botanical studio that feels warm rather than rustic.

## Direction
A deep green field, soft neutral paper tones and a high-contrast wordmark create a calm, editorial tone. The system is deliberately sparse so photography and packaging can carry the atmosphere.

## Deliverables
- Wordmark
- Color system
- Typography pairing
- Packaging concept
- Business card / stationery
- Mini brand guidelines
